# Reference module content and implementation contracts

All names are provisional; themes are product-owner canon. Independent expression is required; theme changes alone are not a legal clearance. Rules fidelity is the test objective. All rules must use shared maker primitives, not host branches checking game names.

| Module | Original theme | Required mechanical scope |
|---|---|---|
| Generation Wars | Satirical generational champions | Legends, champions, main/rune decks, domains, battlefield setup, movement, conquest and hold scoring, contested combat, showdown priority, hidden cards, exhaust/recycle payments |
| Crystal Circuit | Bionic robots and five crystal colors | Land-like crystal sources, color/generic/hybrid/X costs, mana pool, priority/stack, full turn steps, combat and blocking, state-based actions, continuous layers, replacement choices, tokens, counters, alternate faces |
| Looboo | Cute alien creature raising | Active/Bench capacities, basic opening and compensation, evolution timing, attached nutrient costs, multiple attacks, retreat, weakness/resistance, special conditions, knockout badge awards, deck exhaustion |
| Ringworld Rumble | Isekai shonen professional wrestling | Leaders, separate momentum deck, acquisition curve, resource attachment and refresh, active/rested characters, blockers, counters, life triggers, leader colors and life setup, return-resource costs |
| Taste Dimension | Chefs summon and fuse dish monsters | Main/Extra decks, normal/special summons, tribute/material predicates, fusion/synchro/xyz/link/pendulum equivalents, chain speed/optional timing, activation/effect negation, positions and damage, spell/trap zones and face-down effects |

## Content direction
- Generation Wars: Silent Generation, Boomers, Gen X, Millennials, Gen Z and Gen Alpha; satire of cultural habits rather than inherent worth. Example arenas: Open-Plan Office, Family Group Chat, Housing Market, Dead Mall. Initial playable match focuses on Boomers and Gen X; expand other factions.
- Crystal Circuit: azure (analysis/control), ruby (speed/destruction), jade (adaptation/growth), ivory (coordination/protection), obsidian (recycling/attrition). Original robot chassis, crystal sources, upgrades, programs and installations. Existing starter only has a generic resource, so colored economy is a required engine/content upgrade.
- Looboo: alien ecology, nutrient affinities, creature evolution stages, trainers/explorers and field equipment. Give each evolutionary family distinct species and abilities. Existing starter lacks full active/bench and prize procedure.
- Ringworld Rumble: team leaders, wrestlers, managers, arenas, dramatic techniques and counters; momentum resources and resolve/life. Existing starter lacks full leader and resource-deck procedure.
- Taste Dimension: chefs summon dish monsters from ingredients, with kitchen techniques, traps, fusion dishes and other distinct preparation-based summoning systems. Existing starter lacks actual extra-deck fusion procedure.

## Delivery
Build two real decks per game, complete automated matches, portable ZIP replay, card designs, board layout, AI support and graphical authoring. Grow card sets only after the corresponding mechanic is implemented. Test legal and illegal actions and visual binding. Keep evidence catalog 0.2 unchanged; no pass based solely on a theme or starter. Use original art only when generated or supplied for this project.
