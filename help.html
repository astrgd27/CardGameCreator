CARD GAME CREATOR

Workshop handbook

Your first duel

Open New → Blank module. Add a card, give it a name and choose Deal damage. In Game setup set its deck count to six for each player. Set life to six, opening hand to two, energy to three, and draw to one. Start Playtest and click cards in your hand to play them. End turn passes play to George. Return to the workshop and add a second card that restores life. Try the matchup again.

Cards and frames

Place an effect by tapping its tile or dragging it onto the preview. Place replaces the card’s current behavior. Customize changes amounts and adds instructions in order. Script edits the same instructions as text. Try card opens a practice duel with the selected card available immediately. Frame settings reorder the fields on every card. Choose a built-in illustration or upload PNG, JPEG or WebP art.

Units and attacks

Choose Unit in Place to keep the card on the table after its effect resolves. Units start resting and become ready at the beginning of your next turn. Customize sets health and up to four named attacks, each with an energy cost and damage. Choose an attack and target the opponent or an opposing unit. Attacking rests the unit. A unit reduced to zero health goes to its owner’s discard pile.

Table and decks

Choose life, opening hand size, energy per turn, cards drawn, and victory condition in Game setup. Energy refreshes to the set amount at the start of each turn. Reorder the deck, hand, discard and unit field to change how each player's area is displayed. Set each deck’s counts independently. The practice opponent uses the cards in George’s deck. In either victory mode a player who begins a turn with an empty deck loses. Outlast mode disables life-based victory.

Timing and scripts

Main cards are played during their owner’s turn. The opponent may play one affordable reaction before the main card resolves. The threshold checks available energy before payment. Effects run from top to bottom. Life restoration stops at starting life. Drawing from an empty deck during an effect draws zero cards.

Write one instruction per line. The four commands are strike 2 (damage opponent), mend 2 (heal yourself), draw 1, and charge 1 (gain energy). Prefix a command with if_life_below 5 or if_energy_at_least 2 to check your current state at resolution. Amounts are integers 0–99. A card can contain up to 24 instructions.

Rewind and replay

Rewind restores the position before your last action, including George’s replies. Replay deal restarts the current module with the same shuffle seed. Editing the rules or decks ends the old session. An unchanged deck list and seed reproduce the same opening hands.

Saving and sharing

The workshop saves modules in this browser. Open New to switch between saved modules. Export ZIP includes the rules, frame, card catalog, deck lists and art. Import ZIP creates an independent local module. Keep an exported copy when moving devices or clearing browser storage.
